---
title: Pranayama Mantra
---

<div class="vedic-text">
प्राणस्येदं वशे सर्वं त्रिदिवे यत् प्रतिष्ठितम् ।<br>
मातेव पुत्रान् रक्षस्व श्रीश्च प्रज्ञां च विधेहि न इति ॥ २.१३ ॥
</div>

Prāṇasyedaṃ vaśe sarvaṃ tridive yat pratiṣṭhitam ।  
Māteva putrān rakṣasva śrīśca prajñāṃ ca vidhehi na iti ॥ 2.13 ॥

???+ abstract "Source"
    It is the 13th mantra in द्वितीयः प्रश्नः (2nd Chapter) of the प्रश्नोपनिषत् (Prasna Upanisad), अथर्ववेद (Atharva Veda).

???+ tip "Meaning"
    All this is under the control of Prāṇa, including whatever exists in the heavenly worlds. Protect us like a mother protects her children, and grant us prosperity and wisdom.
