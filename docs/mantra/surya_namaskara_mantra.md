title: Surya Namaskara Mantra
---

<div class="vedic-text">
हिरण्मयेन पात्रेण सत्यस्यापिहितं मुखम् । <br>
तत् त्वं पूषन्नपावृणु सत्यधर्माय दृष्टये ॥ १५ ॥
</div>

Hiraṇmayena pātreṇa satyasyāpihitaṃ mukham |  
Tat tvaṃ pūṣann apāvṛṇu satya-dharmāya dṛṣṭaye || 15 ||

???+ abstract "Source" 
    It is the 15th mantra of the ईशोपनिषत् (Isa Upanisad), शुक्लयजुर्वेद (Shukla Yajurveda).


???+ tip "Meaning"
    The face of Truth is covered by a golden vessel. O Pūṣan (the Sun), remove that covering so that I, devoted to Truth, may behold it.
